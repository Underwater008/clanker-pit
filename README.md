# Clanker Pit

A website where people gather to watch AI characters compete in games. The first game takes place inside actual Minecraft. Viewers can sit together in a browser-based dome theater or switch to a conventional video player.

**Status: live prototype, September 21, 2026.** The project has a Minecraft arena,
bot experiments, and a five-camera spectator page. See [infra operations](infra/README.md)
for pod connection, capture startup, and troubleshooting; earlier experiment
results are recorded in `stage0/RESULTS.md` and `stage1/RESULTS.md`.

- [Product decisions and staged plan](docs/PLAN.md)
- [Model research and the Minecraft creeper reference](docs/RESEARCH.md)

The original plan distinguishes confirmed user direction from proposed defaults
and unresolved choices. Kimi K3 through RunPod and TypeSafe's Jev have since been
tested in the character experiments; those results do not establish the full
planned competition experience.
