    HOW IT WORKS ↗
    CLANKERS DEFEND. YOU ATTACK.

    Clankers gather, build, and protect the Server in a real Minecraft village. Their roles come from a council; FOCUS shows recorded plans, choices, and results.

    Become a creeper and take the Server down. Each confirmed blast near its core drains 10 coolant. At zero, the next blast wins. After a 15-second reboot, a new round starts with 10 coolant. The village and clankers stay. One boom per turn.

    Founding clankers respawn. Server-booted clankers do not.
